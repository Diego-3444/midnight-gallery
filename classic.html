Police Briefing

Saturday 3 October · 07:00 · Hollowbrook Museum of Modern Art

At some point on Friday night, The Sleeping Lighthouse by Amara Reyes was taken from Gallery 4. The painting is worth more than the rest of the collection together.

The main alarm never went off. No window was broken. No door was forced. Whoever took the painting walked in, walked out, and used a staff access card to do it.

Only four people hold cards for this wing. All four have given a statement. All four sound honest. One of them is lying, and the lie is in writing.

Your mission: find the thief, find the buyer, and open the loading-bay vault before the museum opens at nine o'clock. You have twenty minutes.

The four keyholders

Dr. Helena Voss
Chief Curator

Rules

Document B — Witness Statements

Taken Saturday 3 October, 07:20–08:05
Dr. Helena Voss
Chief Curator · card #001
I was working late on the autumn catalogue. I did a final check of Gallery 4 at twenty to ten and I locked the connecting door at a quarter to ten. The Reyes was on the wall — I remember straightening the label. Then I drove home. My neighbour, Mrs Haddad, was putting out her bins when I arrived and we talked for a minute. That was just after ten. I have not been back in this building since.
Marcus Oyelaran
Night Security · card #002
My shift started at nine. I walked through Gallery 4 at nine fifty-two and everything was normal — the Reyes was on the wall and I signed the round sheet. At five past ten I heard glass breaking in the east corridor, so I left the gallery to check it. I found nothing broken, nothing at all. I came back about ten minutes later and the painting had gone. I phoned Dr. Voss's office immediately and left a message. After that I was writing my incident report in the security office until the end of my shift.
Priya Raman
Restoration Technician · card #003
I was not even in the city on Friday. My sister got married in Monterrey on Saturday and I flew out on Thursday evening — I can show you the boarding pass. The last time I used my access card was Thursday afternoon, in the restoration room. I have no idea how it could have opened anything on Friday night. I have worked here for six years and I have never lost a card.
Tomás Adler
Gift Shop Manager · card #004
I closed the shop and left through the west staff door at nine thirty-eight — it's in the log, you can check it yourselves. I met my girlfriend at the Cineteca and we watched the nine thirty showing of Low Tide. The film is two hours and ten minutes long, so we were inside that cinema the whole time, from half past nine until twenty to twelve. I came back on Saturday morning and that is when I heard about the painting.
