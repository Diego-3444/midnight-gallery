Police Briefing

Saturday 3 October · 07:00 · Hollowbrook Museum of Modern Art

At some point on Friday night, The Sleeping Lighthouse by Amara Reyes was taken from Gallery 4. The painting is worth more than the rest of the collection together.

The main alarm never went off. No window was broken. No door was forced. Whoever took the painting walked in, walked out, and used a staff access card to do it.

Only four people hold cards for this wing. All four have given a statement. All four sound honest. One of them is lying, and the lie is in writing.

Your mission: find the thief, find the buyer, and open the loading-bay vault before the museum opens at nine o'clock. You have twenty minutes.

Rules
  • Everything is in English. Discuss your reasoning out loud, in English.
  • There are four locks. Each answer opens the next room.

The vault is open.

The Sleeping Lighthouse is inside crate HB-7741, wrapped in a shop apron. It never left the building.

You have the painting. Now close the case — out loud, in English.

The host will ask each of you one of these. Answer in full sentences and say which document or recording proves it.

  1. Who took the painting, and what exactly was wrong with their statement?
  2. Tomás Adler's card only opens the gift shop and the loading bay. So how did he get into Gallery 4, and how do you know?
  3. Priya Raman's card opened Gallery 4 at 22:08. Why is she not the thief?
  4. Who was the buyer, and how did you work out his name from two different documents?
  5. The camera went into "maintenance mode" at 22:30. Who could do that, and why does that matter?
  6. Give one piece of information that you could only get from the voicemail.
