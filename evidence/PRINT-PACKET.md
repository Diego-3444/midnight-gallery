## POLICE BRIEFING
### Saturday 3 October · 07:00

At some point on Friday night, *The Sleeping Lighthouse* by Amara Reyes was taken from
Gallery 4. The painting is worth more than the rest of the collection together.

The main alarm never went off. No window was broken. No door was forced. Whoever took the
painting walked in, walked out, and used a staff access card to do it.

Only four people hold cards for this wing. All four have given a statement. All four sound
honest. One of them is lying, and the lie is in writing.

**Your mission:** find the thief, find the buyer, and open the loading-bay vault before the
museum opens at nine o'clock. You have **twenty minutes**.

**The four keyholders**

- **Dr. Helena Voss** — Chief Curator
- **Marcus Oyelaran** — Night Security
- **Priya Raman** — Restoration Technician
- **Tomás Adler** — Gift Shop Manager

**Rules**

1. Everything is in English. Discuss your reasoning out loud, in English.
2. There are **four locks**. Each answer opens the next piece of evidence.
3. Read every document before you guess. The answer is always in the evidence.
4. You may ask the host for a hint at any time. Hints are free, but we count them.
5. When you open the vault, you must **explain the case out loud**. The vault is not the
   end — the explanation is.
6. One person writes. Everybody talks.

### DOCUMENT B — WITNESS STATEMENTS
**Taken Saturday 3 October, 07:20–08:05**

**Dr. Helena Voss** — *Chief Curator · card #001*

> "I was working late on the autumn catalogue. I did a final check of Gallery 4 at twenty to
> ten and I locked the connecting door at a quarter to ten. The Reyes was on the wall — I
> remember straightening the label. Then I drove home. My neighbour, Mrs Haddad, was putting
> out her bins when I arrived and we talked for a minute. That was just after ten. I have not
> been back in this building since."

**Marcus Oyelaran** — *Night Security · card #002*

> "My shift started at nine. I walked through Gallery 4 at nine fifty-two and everything was
> normal — the Reyes was on the wall and I signed the round sheet. At five past ten I heard
> glass breaking in the east corridor, so I left the gallery to check it. I found nothing
> broken, nothing at all. I came back about ten minutes later and the painting had gone. I
> phoned Dr. Voss's office immediately and left a message. After that I was writing my
> incident report in the security office until the end of my shift."

**Priya Raman** — *Restoration Technician · card #003*

> "I was not even in the city on Friday. My sister got married in Monterrey on Saturday and I
> flew out on Thursday evening — I can show you the boarding pass. The last time I used my
> access card was Thursday afternoon, in the restoration room. I have no idea how it could
> have opened anything on Friday night. I have worked here for six years and I have never
> lost a card."

**Tomás Adler** — *Gift Shop Manager · card #004*

> "I closed the shop and left through the west staff door at nine thirty-eight — it's in the
> log, you can check it yourselves. I met my girlfriend at the Cineteca and we watched the
> nine thirty showing of *Low Tide*. The film is two hours and ten minutes long, so we were
> inside that cinema the whole time, from half past nine until twenty to twelve. I came back
> on Saturday morning and that is when I heard about the painting."

# THE VAULT IS OPEN

*The Sleeping Lighthouse* is inside crate HB-7741, wrapped in a shop apron. It never left
the building.

**Now close the case — out loud, in English.** Answer in full sentences and say **which
document or recording** proves it.

1. Who took the painting, and what exactly was wrong with their statement?
2. Tomás Adler's card only opens the gift shop and the loading bay. So **how did he get into
   Gallery 4**, and how do you know?
3. Priya Raman's card opened Gallery 4 at 22:08. Why is she not the thief?
4. Who was the buyer, and how did you work out his name from two different documents?
5. The camera went into "maintenance mode" at 22:30. Who could do that, and why does it matter?
6. Give one piece of information that you could **only** get from the voicemail.
